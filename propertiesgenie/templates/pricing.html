Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as a "listing"?

Each time you generate a listing, it uses one key. Whether it's a sale, rental, or any property type — it's one key.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. No contracts, no commitments. Cancel anytime and you keep access until the end of your billing period.

Do keys roll over?

No, unused keys reset at the start of each billing cycle. Bonus keys from bundles never expire.

Is there a free trial for paid plans?

The Free plan is your trial! Use 1 listing per month forever. Upgrade only when you're convinced it saves you time.
