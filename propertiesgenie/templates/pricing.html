Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as a "listing"?

Each time you generate a description, it uses one credit. Whether it's a sale, rental, or any property type — it's one credit.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. No contracts, no commitments. Cancel anytime and you keep access until the end of your billing period.

Do credits roll over?

No, unused credits reset at the start of each billing cycle. But with Pro's 100 credits, you'll have plenty.

Is there a free trial for paid plans?

The Free plan is your trial! Use 3 descriptions per month forever. Upgrade only when you're convinced it saves you time.
