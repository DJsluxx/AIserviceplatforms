Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, pages visited.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve the Service.

  • To communicate about your account or respond to inquiries.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share data only with:

  • Law enforcement — when legally required.

4. Data Security

Passwords are hashed. The database is encrypted in transit. We follow industry-standard security practices.

5. Your Rights

You may request your data, update your information, or delete your account by contacting us.

6. Cookies

We use session cookies to keep you logged in. No third-party tracking cookies.
