Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about keys and subscriptions

What’s a “key”?

Each key unlocks one complete listing — including a title, multi-paragraph copy, and key highlights. One key = one listing.

Do bought keys expire?

No! One-time key bundles never expire. They stay in your account until you use them. Subscription keys refresh monthly.

Can I buy keys AND have a subscription?

Yes! Your subscription keys are used first each month. Bonus keys from bundles are only consumed when subscription keys run out.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Absolutely. Cancel anytime with no penalties. You keep access until the end of your billing period, and any purchased key bundles remain yours.

Is the annual plan worth it?

If you plan to use Properties Genie for more than a few months, the annual plan saves you up to 36%. Most active agents choose annual.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept PayPal and all major credit/debit cards through PayPal's secure checkout. No card info is stored on our servers.
