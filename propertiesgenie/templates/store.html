Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about credits and subscriptions

What's a "credit"?

Each credit generates one complete listing description — including a title, multi-paragraph description, and key highlights. One credit = one listing.

Do bought credits expire?

No! One-time credit packs never expire. They stay in your account until you use them. Subscription credits refresh monthly.

Can I buy credits AND have a subscription?

Yes! Your subscription credits are used first each month. Bonus credits from packs are only consumed when subscription credits run out.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Absolutely. Cancel anytime with no penalties. You keep access until the end of your billing period, and any purchased credit packs remain yours.

Is the annual plan worth it?

If you plan to use Properties Genie for more than a few months, the annual plan saves you up to 36%. Most active agents choose annual.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept PayPal and all major credit/debit cards through PayPal's secure checkout. No card info is stored on our servers.
